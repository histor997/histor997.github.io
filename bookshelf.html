Bookshelf

← Back to portfolio

Leo Strauss, Persecution and the Art of Writing — Ended my hiding.
Robert Caro, The Power Broker — Taught me that building is never neutral.
James Scott, Seeing Like a State — Why legibility is a form of violence.
Benjamin Elman, On Their Own Terms — How China received Western science on Chinese terms, not the other way around.
Elizabeth Perry, Shanghai on Strike — The guild paper's afterlife. How radical artisans were transformed into pioneers of Chinese communism.
Yü Ying-shih, The Religious Ethic and Mercantile Spirit in Early Modern China — The Weber question, answered from the other side.
Eric Foner, Reconstruction — The American experiment at its most fragile. What it costs to extend the shot.
Bryan Stevenson, Just Mercy — What rightful resistance looks like inside a courtroom.
Feeding the Machine — The book that theorizes from outside what I entered from within.
Gloria Anzaldúa, Borderlands/La Frontera — In-betweenness as intellectual condition, not biographical accident.
Rutilius Namatianus, De Reditu Suo — A man watching Rome recede from the stern of a boat. I read it while my own empire was receding.
Aristophanes, Clouds — The first comedy about intellectuals who think they're too smart for the city. Still accurate.
Dostoevsky, Crime and Punishment — The extreme version of a thesis-oriented life.
Vannevar Bush, Science: The Endless Frontier — The memo that built postwar American science. Infrastructure as the argument.
Thomas Kuhn, The Structure of Scientific Revolutions — Paradigm shifts happen in funerals.
Peter Hopkirk, The Great Game — Imperial rivalry played out in passes and deserts. Geopolitics before anyone called it that.
Kenneth Pomeranz, The Great Divergence — Why Europe industrialized and China didn't. The question behind the question behind the guild paper.
Marc Andreessen, "The Techno-Optimist Manifesto" — The case for building as moral imperative. I disagree with parts of it. I carry all of it.
Søren Kierkegaard, Fear and Trembling — Abraham's leap. Conviction without evidence, action without guarantee. The leap that precedes the landing.
